Findings

01highLikely contradictionhigh confidence

Pricing page bills on active users. FAQ says total subscribers.

Pricing page

Active users, last 30 days

Subscribed users or people with an event in the last 30 days.
bentonow.com/pricing

FAQ

Every unique email address

Bento charges based on the number of subscribers you have.
bentonow.com/docs/faq
Impact
On a list with a long dormant tail those two rules produce different invoices. It is also the reason a prospect picks you over the competitors in your own table.
Check
The FAQ is probably older copy from before the Active Users model. It is still the page customers search when they want to know what drives their bill.

02mediumAmbiguitymedium confidence

FAQ says nothing is gated. Pricing page sells chat at $30 a month.

FAQ

No feature gating at any tier

All features are included at every tier
bentonow.com/docs/faq

Pricing page

Chat costs $30 a month extra

+$30/mo adds shared inbox, live chat, SMS, routing, saved replies, and AI agents. Requires Marketing Platform.
bentonow.com/pricing
Impact
Someone who reads the FAQ first budgets $29 and is surprised at $59.
Check
Tier almost certainly means volume tier, and Chat is arguably a separate product. Four words in the FAQ would settle it.

Definitions

Likely contradiction: Two public statements that cannot both be true.

Ambiguity: Both statements can be true, but a customer cannot tell what they get.
