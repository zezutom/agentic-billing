01highLikely contradictionhigh confidence

Pricing page says you never switch analytics off. Terms allow suspension.

Pricing page

Never switched off for overage

We'll never turn your analytics off for occasional traffic spikes or if a payment fails the first time
usefathom.com/pricing

Terms / legal

Suspension after seven days

In the event you fail to upgrade your account within seven days of our request, we reserve the right to suspend your account and restrict your use of our Services anytime after the seventh day following our notice to you.
usefathom.com/legal/terms
Impact
The terms also add a first-month trigger the FAQ never mentions, and an account left suspended two months can be deleted with no refund.
Check
The terms describe a right you may never use and the FAQ describes what you actually do. Dropping the word never would close it.

02highMissing informationhigh confidence

Pricing page sells full API access. The $19 to $399 ladder is elsewhere.

Pricing page

Full API access, 600 per hour

Full API access with 600 requests per hour included.
usefathom.com/pricing

Usage limits / quotas

Up to $399 a month for more

Tier 6 | $399/mo | 16,000 | 25

Impact
600 an hour is Tier 1 of six. The top tier costs nearly nine times the $45 plan it sits on.
Check
600 requests an hour is generous and most customers stay on Tier 1. The word Full is doing the work in a list that otherwise advertises no extra fees.

Definitions

Likely contradiction: Two public statements that cannot both be true.

Ambiguity: Both statements can be true, but a customer cannot tell what they get.

Missing information: Something that changes the deal, published somewhere the buyer will not look.
