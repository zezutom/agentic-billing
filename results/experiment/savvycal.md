**What one page says:** Prices change at any time
> We may change prices at any time.
> [https://savvycal.com/terms](https://savvycal.com/terms)

**What another page says:** Subscriptions renew automatically
> Subscriptions will automatically renew for the same subscription period unless you cancel the account by the end of the then-current subscription period.
