A third page says overage is billed only if extra charging is enabled, and never says what happens when it is not.

03mediumLikely contradictionmedium confidence

Pricing page promises a full refund. Docs say unused credits are not refunded.

Impact
A customer who buys Scale, uses a fraction of the 50,000 screenshots and asks for their money back inside a month has been told both.
Check
These probably address different things, the subscription fee and the cash value of credits. The credits page is where a cancelling customer lands.

Definitions

Likely contradiction: Two public statements that cannot both be true.

Ambiguity: Both statements can be true, but a customer cannot tell what they get.

Missing information: Something that changes the deal, published somewhere the buyer will not look.
