Soft limits, spend can exceed

not instantaneous hard caps. Usage is evaluated on a short delay, so spend can briefly exceed your limit before enforcement applies.

Spend can exceed the limit before enforcement applies, and the pricing page does not say so. Anyone setting a cap to protect a budget is relying on the stronger reading.

04mediumMissing informationmedium confidence

The Free plan stops at $5 of credits. The limits page omits it.

Pricing page

Free stops at $5 of credits

On the Free plan, you'll need to upgrade to keep running tasks once the included $5 of credits is used.
trigger.dev/pricing

Usage limits / quotas

Spend caps are not a platform limit

Looking to cap your monthly spend? That's a setting you control, not a platform limit
trigger.dev/docs/limits
Impact
That $5 is the ceiling a free user actually hits. The page titled Limits says a spend cap is your setting rather than a platform limit, which is not true on Free.
Check
The limits page sentence is about the billing limits feature rather than about free credits, so the conflict is one of placement more than of fact.

Definitions

Likely contradiction: Two public statements that cannot both be true.

Ambiguity: Both statements can be true, but a customer cannot tell what they get.

Missing information: Something that changes the deal, published somewhere the buyer will not look.
