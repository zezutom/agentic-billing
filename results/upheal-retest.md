## What you appear to sell

- **Free**: $0
- **Premium**: $1 per session, capped at $69 a month
- **Group plan**: no published price
- **Custom Enterprise plan**: Contact Sales
- **Starter (legacy)**: $29 a month, or $19 billed annually
- **Premium (legacy)**: $99 a month, or $69 billed annually
- **Managed insurance billing**: 5.9% of collections

## 1. You say you charge for work done. Scheduling alone is billable.

`high` `Ambiguity` `high confidence`

Book 40 appointments, write 20 notes, pay for 40. Telehealth calls count too, with or without a note.

**What one page says:** Charge for jobs done, not access
> Legacy EHRs charge for access. We charge for jobs done.
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

**What another page says:** Scheduling counts, note or not
> A session is counted each time you generate an AI note or schedule a session with a client
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

*Why this is not just wording: Scheduling is access, and access is the thing the headline says you do not charge for.*

*What would make this a non-issue: The $69 cap bounds it, and the same answer says the fee covers the whole platform rather than the note.*

## 2. Free claims offer runs on four pages. One of them says solo only.

`high` `Missing information` `high confidence`

Group practices file the most claims and are the ones putting the wrong number in a budget.

**What one page says:** $0.30 per claim, free till September
> $0.30 per claim submission, $0.15 per eligibility check
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

**What another page says:** Solo practices only
> Applicable only for solo practicies.
> [https://www.upheal.io/features/insurance-billing](https://www.upheal.io/features/insurance-billing)

*Why this is not just wording: Three places state the offer with no limit and one limits it to a subset of customers.*

*What would make this a non-issue: Four words on the pricing page fixes it. The restriction line also has a typo, so it looks like a late addition.*

**What one page says:** No added cost, no plan named
> Built into Upheal at no added cost.
> [https://www.upheal.io/features/treatment-plans](https://www.upheal.io/features/treatment-plans)

## What we could not check

Your support centre is the main gap. The collection pages we reached list article titles and nothing else, so none of the 186 help articles behind them was read. Account management, Client payments and Insurance billing are the three collections most likely to settle the findings above. upheal.io/terms returns a 404 from our crawl, so renewal, refund and price-change terms were not reviewed at all. The Group and Custom Enterprise plans publish no prices, so team pricing could not be checked for consistency. The plan comparison tables on all three pricing pages are built from styled divs rather than real tables, so per-plan values lose their column alignment when flattened to text. Nothing reported above depends on reading them.
