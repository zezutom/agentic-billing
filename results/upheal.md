# Upheal: commercial consistency audit

## What you appear to sell

- **Free**: $0
- **Premium (usage-based)**: $1 /session, capped at $69/month
- **Group plan**: not published; webinar or trial signup
- **Custom Enterprise plan**: Contact Sales
- **Starter (legacy, flat-rate)**: $29/month, or $19/month billed annually
- **Premium (legacy, flat-rate)**: $99/month, or $69/month billed annually
- **Managed insurance billing**: 5.9% of collections

## 1. Your headline says you charge for work done. Scheduling alone is billable.

`high` `Ambiguity` `high confidence`

A therapist who schedules 40 appointments and writes 20 notes pays for 40.

**What one page says:** Charges for jobs done, not access
> Legacy EHRs charge for access. We charge for jobs done.
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

**What another page says:** Billable on scheduling, note or not
> A session is counted each time you generate an AI note or schedule a session with a client
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

*Why this is not just wording: Scheduling is access, which is the thing the headline says you do not charge for.*

*What would make this a non-issue: The $69 monthly cap limits the damage, and the same answer explains that the fee covers the whole platform.*

## 2. Free claims offer is advertised to everyone, restricted to solo practices in one line.

`high` `Missing information` `high confidence`

Group practices submit the most claims and are the ones budgeting on the wrong number.

**What one page says:** $0.30 per claim, free till end of September
> $0.30 per claim submission, $0.15 per eligibility check
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

**What another page says:** Solo practices only
> Applicable only for solo practicies.
> [https://www.upheal.io/features/insurance-billing](https://www.upheal.io/features/insurance-billing)

*Why this is not just wording: Three places state the offer with no restriction and one place limits it to a subset of customers.*

*What would make this a non-issue: Four words on the pricing page would fix it. The restriction line also contains a typo, so it looks like a late addition.*

## 3. Trial is 30 days on the pricing page, 14 days on the pricing reference.

`high` `Likely contradiction` `medium confidence`

A practice manager comparing tools gets two answers depending on which page they land on.

**What one page says:** 30 days
> 30-day full trial
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

**What another page says:** 14 days, teams
> 14-day full-featured trial
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing-reference](https://www.upheal.io/pricing-reference)

*Why this is not just wording: Both pages present their number as the trial a team would get.*

*What would make this a non-issue: The 14-day figure sits on a page headed Legacy plans, so it may be out of date. Nothing on the page marks where legacy ends.*

## 4. Canadian page prices card fees in C$ and claim fees in US$.

`medium` `Likely contradiction` `high confidence`

**What one page says:** 2.9% + C$0.30 per card charge
> Low fees for card transactions: 2.9% + C$0.30
> [https://www.upheal.io/en-ca/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/en-ca/pricing)

**What another page says:** $0.30 per claim, no C
> $0.30 per claim submission, $0.15 per eligibility check
> [https://www.upheal.io/en-ca/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/en-ca/pricing)

*Why this is not just wording: The page converts every other price on it, so a bare dollar sign reads as a different currency.*

*What would make this a non-issue: Looks like a localisation pass that missed the insurance billing block.*

## 5. Premium is both your current tier and a discontinued $99 plan.

`medium` `Possibly out of date` `high confidence`

A prospect searching for the price of Premium finds $99, $69, or $1 per session capped at $69.

**What one page says:** Current tier, $1 per session
> you won't be able to use features only included in our Premium plan
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

**What another page says:** Legacy plan, $99 per month
> $99 $69 /month billed annually
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing-reference](https://www.upheal.io/pricing-reference)

*Why this is not just wording: One plan name carries two prices and two billing models.*

*What would make this a non-issue: Your FAQ says outright that pricing changed and links to the legacy page, which is more than most companies do.*

## 6. One offer names a month that has passed. Another names no year.

`low` `Possibly out of date` `medium confidence`

A reader cannot tell from either page whether the offer is still open.

**What one page says:** Beta opens August 2026, now past
> Closed beta opens August 2026
> [https://www.upheal.io/features/managed-insurance-billing](https://www.upheal.io/features/managed-insurance-billing)

**What another page says:** Free till September, no year
> Free till end of September
> [https://www.upheal.io/pricing](https://www.upheal.io/pricing)

*Why this is not just wording: Both are statements about when an offer applies, and neither resolves to a date a reader can act on.*

*What would make this a non-issue: The beta may have opened on time and the wording simply not been updated.*

The plan comparison tables are built from styled divs rather than real tables, so per-plan values lose their column alignment when flattened to text. Nothing above depends on reading them.
